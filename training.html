Hands-on Training and Interview Briefings for Skilled Deployment

Hands-on training at Global HR brings classroom instruction together with real interview briefings, so candidates understand both the technical work and the standards employers expect on site.

Sessions range from theory tests and safety instruction to trade briefings ahead of client interviews, helping skilled workers from Myanmar and across the region present themselves with confidence.

These programmes sit alongside internationally recognised certification pathways, so trained candidates are ready for mobilisation onto oil & gas, construction, marine and industrial projects.
