Our story

Since 2001, Global HR has been helping employers build reliable workforces across Asia-Pacific and beyond.

With recruitment experience spanning Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Japan, South Korea, Dubai, Vietnam and other regional markets, we connect employers with the right talent through a combination of local market knowledge, established recruitment networks and structured, compliant processes.

Over the years, we have supported businesses across diverse industries and workforce requirements—from individual skilled hires to large-scale manpower recruitment for projects and operations.

At Global HR, we understand that successful recruitment is about more than filling vacancies. It is about understanding each employer's operational needs, identifying suitable candidates, managing recruitment and deployment effectively, and building relationships based on reliability, transparency and results.

Today, Global HR brings more than two decades of recruitment experience to every partnership—helping employers find the right people, build stronger teams and move their businesses forward.

Our Vision

To transform lives through skills, employment and opportunity.

We bring together people seeking a better future and employers seeking the skilled workforce to build theirs—creating opportunities that improve livelihoods, strengthen businesses and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Mission

To connect businesses with the skilled workforce they need to succeed, while creating meaningful employment opportunities that improve lives and livelihoods.

Through responsible recruitment, regional expertise and trusted partnerships, we create lasting value for employers, people and the communities we serve.
