About Global HR

People-first recruitment across two markets

Our story

Global HR was built to connect Myanmar talent with Singapore opportunities—and to help employers in both countries hire with confidence. We combine local market knowledge with structured processes so every placement is clear, compliant, and built to last.

Whether you are hiring your next team member or taking the next step in your career, we focus on communication, respect, and outcomes you can measure.

Our values

Integrity

Transparent processes and honest guidance for candidates and clients.

Speed with care

We move quickly, without cutting corners on screening or compliance.

Partnership

We work alongside employers and job seekers as a long-term ally.

Quality matches

We prioritise fit: skills, culture, and career direction—not volume alone.
