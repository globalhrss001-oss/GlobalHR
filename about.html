Our story

Global HR has supported recruitment across Asia-Pacific & beyond since 2001. We connect talent with opportunities in China, India, and the wider region—and help employers hire with confidence, combining local market knowledge with structured, compliant processes.

Whether you are hiring your next team member or taking the next step in your career, we focus on communication, respect, and outcomes you can measure.
