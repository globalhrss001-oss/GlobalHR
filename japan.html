Japan recruitment

Global HR sources suitable workers from Myanmar for Japanese employers and supervising organisations through recognised occupation and skills-development programmes.

Recruitment pathways

TITP and Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) have different eligible occupations and rules. They are listed separately.

Technical Intern Training Program (TITP)

A Japan-specific training and skills-development route with its own official list of occupations and work activities. Eligibility is set by Japanese authorities and the supervising organisation — not by this website.

View TITP job categories

Specified Skilled Worker (SSW)

A separate skilled-worker status with its own fields and tests. SSW occupations are not the same as the TITP list and will not be mixed on that page.
