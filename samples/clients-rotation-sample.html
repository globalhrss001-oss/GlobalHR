Rotation Design Samples

Two ways to rotate 50+ client logos in a compact space

Style 1 — Continuous Marquee

Two rows scroll in opposite directions, looping forever. Hover to pause. Great as a homepage "trusted by" band.

Style 2 — Fade-Swap Rotation

A fixed set of slots; each tile quietly fades out and a new client fades in on a timer, so all 50+ rotate through a small area.

Standalone sample. Colored monogram tiles stand in for real logos. Both styles pause/disable for users with “reduced motion” enabled, and the marquee pauses on hover for accessibility.
